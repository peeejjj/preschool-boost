Preschool Boost with AkindaCo

We have three programs on the OECD Preschool Boost Menu. Book one, or combine them. Every price is per session, not per person, and we come to your centre.

Send a booking request

The three programs

1. Learn to Play

A therapist-led pretend play group in your room, based on Dr Karen Stagnitti’s Learn to Play approach. It builds the play skills that sit under language, social development and school readiness, and it teaches your educators to run the group themselves.

Who
8 to 10 children and two of your educators
What
A weekly two-hour visit: 30 minutes with educators, a 60-minute group with the children, 30 minutes of reflection
How long
17 program weeks plus a set-up visit. We can pause over school holidays if desired
Price
$570 + GST per visit in 2026, $591 in 2027

2. ATCD Training

Attachment, Trauma and Child Development training for educators. Six units that build your team’s skills in reading children’s behaviour, creating safety, co-regulation and working with families.

Who
Your whole team. Any number of educators for the one price
What
Six 3-hour units, online or in your centre. Book the full series or single units
How long
Usually one unit a fortnight across a term
Price
$855 + GST per unit in 2026, $887 in 2027

The units: 1. Foundations of attachment and development. 2. Understanding trauma in the early years. 3. Creating safe and predictable environments. 4. Co-regulation and emotional literacy. 5. Embedding therapeutic practice into daily routines. 6. Engaging families through an attachment lens.

3. Allied Health Supports

Our therapists work in your centre with groups of children and the educators around them: observing, running small groups, modelling strategies and coaching your team. We plan it with you around a goal you set.

Who
Groups of children and educators
What
Visits shaped to your centre’s goals
How long
As many hours as your allocation allows
Price
$194 + GST per hour in 2026, $201 in 2027

How to book

  1. Send a booking request. It takes about five minutes.
  2. We send a quote and hold your dates within two business days.
  3. Confirm by email once your Preschool Boost plan is approved, and we invoice you.

Would you rather talk first? Book a free 30-minute call.

What we ask of your centre

  • Learn to Play: a play space, help choosing the children, and the same two educators each week, who stay in the group for the full two hours.
  • ATCD Training: a room with a screen, and educators released for the three hours of each unit.
  • All programs: a day and time that works for your centre, arranged with us at booking, and as much notice as you can if a session cannot go ahead.

Questions centres ask us

Is the price per educator?

No. Every price is per session for your whole team.

Is the 17 weeks for all programs?

No. Only Learn to Play runs for 17 program weeks. ATCD Training is six units, and you can book just one.

Can ATCD Training run after hours or online?

Yes. Units can run in the evening, in a staff meeting slot or during the day, online or in your centre. Learn to Play is always in your room.

Can we hold dates before our plan is approved?

Yes. Nothing is invoiced until you confirm in writing.

What if a session is cancelled?

If we cancel, we make it up at no charge. If your centre cancels with less than two business days’ notice, the session is charged.

What do we get at the end?

CPD certificates for educators, and for Learn to Play an end-of-program summary you can use in your reporting.

Can Allied Health Supports be used for one child?

No. Preschool Boost funds work with groups and educators, not individual therapy.
